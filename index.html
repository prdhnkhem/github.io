Life & Purpose

Welcome. I believe everything in life is connected, and I have spent my years searching for the quiet connections this mainstream system keeps hidden. I am an independent researcher and a life companion from Damak, Nepal.

Work with me See my journey
Everything is Connected

Space and earth. Past, present, and future.
Humans, nature, and technology. None of it stands apart.

I work where these threads meet. When one falls out of balance, the others feel it. So I do not try to fix a life, a body, a home, or a business in pieces. We read the whole, and bring it back into harmony.

Humans

Nature

Technology

I have lived these things,
not only read them.

The world we live in is loud and built for profit, not for the growth of people. I call it the failing social system, and most of us are too busy surviving it to notice where it is breaking us.

My life has been one long research project into what it means to be human. I left the path I was handed, found my own books and teachers, travelled, lived among many communities, and worked across yoga, Ayurveda, permaculture, Vastu, astrology, business, and building with my hands. I returned with what actually works.

My goal is simple. To bring everything I have lived and found into one work, helping humans, nature, and technology grow together in peace and balance. Whatever problem you carry, we find the root, design the change, and stay with it until it is fixed in your life.

Khem Raj Pradhan, Damak

Act I

The Village

Roots in the hills, and the choice to leave the path I was handed.

The Hills
Personal

Born in Bhojpur

I was born in the hills of Bhojpur, into a Pradhan Newar and Parajuli Brahmin family. My first world was mud walls, animals, and a village that raised everyone together.

Childhood
Personal

We moved to Damak

In time we came down to Damak, a bigger town with roads, electricity, and a faster kind of life. A new world for a hill child.

The Hostel Years
Personal

Twelve years in hostel

From a young age I lived in a hostel. Twelve years in a small, isolated world that taught me to watch, to wait, and to stand on my own.

2009 to 2011
Personal

Training for the British Army

For three years I trained hard to join the British Gurkhas. The discipline stayed with me, even after the path changed.

Leaving School
Personal

I left school to find my own teachers

I tried Science, then Management, then Arts, and none of it held me. I left formal school because I could not relate what they taught to what my life was actually asking of me. So I decided to find my own teachers and my own books.

Act II

The Descent and the Turn

The dark years, a friend lost, and the first steps back toward the light.

The Dark Phase
Personal

Addiction, and a friend lost

Then came the dark years. Addiction took hold, and it took a friend from me. I do not hide this part. It is where the search truly began.

Learning to Speak
Personal

Nights on the US call floor

I joined US based call centers and worked nights from Nepal. I sold websites, gathered leads, and rented out construction equipment, all to people across America. I learned to speak, to sell, and to understand the United States and its culture, one call at a time.

The Turn
Travel

Ten days of silence

I decided to change, and I started to move. Vipassana gave me ten days of silence, and the first clear look inward.

The Road
Travel

Life on the truck, as a khalashi

To see my own country, I worked on trucks as a khalashi. I read and wrote along the way and reached places I would never have found otherwise. I slept on highways and inside truck cabins. Noise, pollution, corruption, trade, the women who work the roads, and raw nature. I saw many things here.
