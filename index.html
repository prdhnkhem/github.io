01  ·  About

A Companion for Your Life Journey

The world we live in is loud, confusing, and manipulative because most of its systems are built for profit and control, not for the unity and evolution of people. I call it a Failing Social System. And most of us are too busy surviving it to notice where it is breaking us.

I am Khem Raj Pradhan, from a small village in the hills of Nepal. My life has been a research project into what it means to be human, moving through doubt, loss, and darkness, and finding my way back to purpose, balance, and clarity. Not once, but many times.

From an early age, I chose a different path. I left behind the implanted desires, found my own books and teachers, travelled, broke rules, lived among different communities, and worked across many fields and cultures. I learned life the inconvenient way, and now I want to share what I found.

I offer my time, experience, and hand as a companion to those ready to step away from the noise and build a simpler, more natural life.

My goal is simple. Evolution over Degradation. Whatever you are facing in your life, your body, or your work, I want to work through it with you, as a friend, find the root cause, and help you move forward. You do not have to do it alone.

Help I Can Provide

My work adapts to who you are and what you need. Below you will find exactly what I can offer, broken down by who I work with.

For Individuals

You are one person navigating life, feeling stuck, lost, or simply wanting to grow. Whether the friction is internal (your mindset, habits, body, or purpose) or external (your space, relationships, or direction), I work alongside you as a trusted companion to find the root causes and move toward a better version of your life.

  1. Deep conversation partner. Talk about anything without judgment, philosophy, religion, politics, love, trauma, sexuality, spirituality, conspiracies, darkness, visions, or goals. I listen, reflect, and help you think more clearly.
  2. Daily life companion. Share a meal, a walk, a coffee, a shopping trip, or a day out. Sometimes what a person needs is simply someone present and engaged, not advice, just genuine company.
  3. Health, routine & Ayurveda. I help you align with the rhythms of nature, designing daily routines, timetables, seasonal eating habits, micro-habits, and time management practices rooted in Ayurvedic principles.
  4. Astrology & self-understanding. I help you understand your birth chart, astrological patterns, and what they reveal about your character, strengths, blind spots, and life direction.
  5. Vastu Shastra. I consult on and redesign your living space using Vastu principles, adjusting the flow, direction, and arrangement of your home or room to bring balance and harmony to your daily environment.
  6. Yoga, meditation & physical practice. I can be your yoga teacher, meditation guide, or massage partner. I also train alongside you in football, MMA, wrestling, jujutsu, swimming, basketball, or any physical discipline, as both companion and teacher.
  7. Outdoor & adventure companion. I guide and accompany you on hikes, treks, camping trips, fishing, and travel, teaching practical skills along the way and helping you reconnect with nature.
  8. Personal brand & digital presence. I help you build a personal brand, manage social media, create a website, and design a clear, authentic public identity that reflects who you truly are.
  9. Life design & purpose. I help you understand your body type, identify your purpose, clarify your future, and build a realistic lifestyle plan to achieve your vision, step by step, without overwhelm.
  10. Hands-on & DIY. I help with practical home tasks, basic plumbing, carpentry, painting, welding, and building projects. Useful, grounding, and often therapeutic.

For Families

When the whole family is ready to work together, I come in as a neutral observer, facilitator, and guide. I help families solve deep-rooted problems, build unity, distribute responsibilities clearly, and design a shared vision for the future, whether that involves a home, a garden, a family business, or simply a healthier way of living together.

  1. Family problem-solving & mediation. I observe family dynamics, identify recurring conflicts and unspoken frictions, and facilitate honest conversations that lead to real resolution, not temporary compromise.
  2. Unity & responsibility design. I help distribute roles and responsibilities among family members in a way that suits each person's strengths, reducing resentment and building genuine cooperation.
  3. Home & garden design. I design homes using eco-architecture and eco-partition principles, and create permaculture gardens, urban food systems, or regenerative outdoor spaces for the whole family to benefit from.
  4. Family business planning. I help families plan, organise, and operate a shared business, aligning vision, clarifying roles, and building systems that prevent the common tensions that arise when family and business mix.
  5. Future planning together. I guide the family in building a shared long-term plan, for life, finances, land, education, and community, rooted in natural law and regenerative principles.

For Businesses

I enter a business the way a researcher enters a system, observing quietly, mapping friction, and identifying what is draining energy, money, and morale. I work as an insider observer, not an outsider consultant. My approach is to understand your business from within, then help restructure it into something ethical, efficient, and sustainable.

  1. Business observation & friction mapping. I spend time inside your business, watching operations, team interactions, and workflows, to identify losses, loopholes, redundancies, and hidden drains that numbers alone do not reveal.
  2. Team training & culture. I work directly with your team to improve communication, accountability, and working culture, removing the noise that slows people down and replacing it with clarity and shared purpose.
  3. Branding, marketing & social media. I help design your brand identity, build a marketing strategy, manage social media presence, produce video content, and teach your team to run it independently.
  4. Ethical & regenerative business design. For new or existing businesses, I help you build a profitable model without being exploitative, designing operations, supply chains, and customer relationships rooted in natural law and long-term thinking.
  5. Supplier, vendor & market research. I research and identify the right suppliers, vendors, and market opportunities, helping you make informed decisions rather than reactive ones.

For Non-Profits & Social Movements

If you are running a community initiative, social project, or grassroots movement with a genuine mission, I offer my time, research skills, and hands as a companion and collaborator. I believe the future belongs to people who are trying to build something better, and I want to support that work in whatever way is most useful.

  1. Research & planning partner. I help non-profits and movements research their cause deeply, understand the systems they are trying to change, and build a clear, grounded strategy for long-term impact.
  2. Community design & event organisation. I assist in organising events, gatherings, and community activities, from concept and logistics to facilitation and follow-up.
  3. Content, filming & storytelling. I help document your work through video, social media, and written content, giving your mission a clear and compelling voice that reaches the right people.
  4. Regenerative infrastructure. For community land or spaces, I help design permaculture systems, eco-architecture, and regenerative food production to make the project self-sustaining over time.

Note. All services I provide are natural law-based and regenerative. I care deeply about humanity, nature, technology, and most importantly, the future. Everything is rooted in balance and harmony. I will only provide paths, solutions, and advice that will not harm human evolution or nature.

So much of my advice may be radical, the bitter truth, and far from mainstream teachings and lifestyle. Be ready for it.

03  ·  Process

How the Process Works

Four phases. One clear path toward truth and transformation.

01

Deconstruction. The Truth.

We start by fully reading and understanding this website together. Then you fill out the form to book an hour-long video call with me. If the problem is solved in that call, good. If things are still unclear, we arrange a short week-long visit to your place.

02

Identification. The Friction.

I live closely with you for a week, observing your daily routines, environments, foods, timetables, the people you meet, work patterns, body postures, sleeping and eating habits, and everything in between. Do not worry. That is what you hired me for. I will be your best friend and advisor, making it easy and comfortable.

03

Design. The Blueprint.

After a week, I suggest small, specific changes, one at a time. Nothing overwhelming. Only what is truly necessary to shift the direction of your life toward evolution. Every recommendation is rooted in natural law, Ayurveda, spatial intelligence, and systems thinking.

04

Implementation. The Reality.

If the process requires more time, I continue staying with you for as long as you need. The journey does not end at a plan. It ends when the change is real and felt in your daily life.

04  ·  Working With Me

Ethics, Terms & How I Work

I work with people who are genuinely ready to change. The work I do is not for everyone, and it is not designed to be easy. It is designed to be true.

I will be present, honest, and direct with you. I will not soften the message to make you feel comfortable, and I will not stretch the engagement to extract more fees. If something will not work for you, I will tell you. If we are not the right fit, I will say so before we begin.

I expect the same in return. Honest conversation. Real effort. A genuine intention to change.

A note on commitment. This work requires genuine effort from both sides. If you choose not to follow more than three of the agreed recommendations, whether advice, habit changes, or practical adjustments, the engagement will need to end. This is not a punishment. It is a boundary that protects both your time and mine.

Fees are non-refundable once the work has begun. I ask only that you come with an open mind and a real intention to change.

The Hunate Movement

The world we inherited is broken. Its education systems, economic models, political structures, and relationship with nature are all failing, not by accident, but by design.

The Hunate Movement exists to question that design, find what went wrong, and build something better.

We are not an NGO. Not a company. Not a political party. We are simply humans, researchers, builders, thinkers, farmers, engineers, healers, coming together without registration, without hierarchy, without agenda, to do what humans are supposed to do. Learn the truth, share it freely, and build a life worth living.

Our three pillars are simple. Humans. Nature. Technology. In balance. In harmony. In the service of life, not profit.

We are in the early stages. The movement is growing quietly. If something in you recognises what we are saying, you already belong here.
