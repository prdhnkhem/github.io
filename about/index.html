:: THE BIOPRINT ::

My life is not a biography. It is a series of laboratory experiments.

I was born into a rebel Newari-Brahmin family in the hills of Nepal. My upbringing wasn't standard—it was a collision of Hindu and Buddhist heritage, set against the backdrop of civil war and family incarceration. I didn't grow up in a living room; I grew up observing the world from the outside.

How I learned to see the patterns

1. THE LABORATORY OF ISOLATION (1991 - 2011)

I spent 12 years in the strict discipline of a Christian hostel, far from my home. Being an "outsider" within different religious and social groups taught me to look for the rules that run people’s lives. While others were fitting in, I was taking notes on the systems they were following.

2. THE LABORATORY OF INDUSTRY (2012 - 2023)

I have worked in almost every sector of human commerce. From the high-pressure dopamine of US call centers and the luxury of 5-star resorts to the raw, physical survival of Himalayan sawmills and lumber harvesting. I hitchhiked without money and volunteered on remote organic farms in Cambodia. I have been the addict, the laborer, the Principal of a school, and a companion to travelers from all over the world.

3. THE DETACHMENT (PRESENT)

After seeing how the mainstream "profit-economy" breaks the human spirit and destroys nature, I chose to leave the race. I detached myself from social norms to become a bias-free researcher. I have dated and lived with people from every corner of the globe—white, black, rich, poor, smart, and unlearned. This diversity has taught me the one thing we all have in common: we are all living in systems that need redesigning.
