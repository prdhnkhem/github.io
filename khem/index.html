01 · About

I have lived these things,
not only read them.

I am Khem Raj Pradhan, from a small village in the hills of Bhojpur, Nepal, and raised in Damak. From early on I chose a different path. I left the desires the system handed me, found my own books and teachers, travelled, broke rules, and lived among many communities.

My real classroom has been the world of work itself. I have lived in a yoga ashram and on a permaculture farm, taught in schools and cared for children in orphanages, waited tables in a five star hotel, run a saw mill and a furniture and lumber business, and sold for international call centres. I have managed transport and logistics, advised businesses, led projects and teams, guided people through fitness and Ayurvedic routines, worked on eco architecture projects, and organised social and charity events across Nepal. I did not only read about these things. I tested them in my own body and life, and came back with what actually works.

I offer my time, experience, and hand as a companion to those ready to step away from the noise and build a simpler, more natural life. My goal is balance. We find the root, design the change, and stay with it until it is real in your life.

02 · Story

The long way round

I learned life the inconvenient way. A few of the turns that shaped the work.

The Hills

Born in Bhojpur

Born into a Pradhan Newar and Parajuli Brahmin family in the hills, then carried down to Damak as a child, between old village rhythms and a fast changing town.

The Hostel Years

Twelve years away from home

From a young age I lived in a hostel, a small and isolated world. It taught me to watch closely, wait patiently, and stand on my own. The fuller story is in my personal journey book.

The Turning

I left the path

Around the end of school I chose to step off the planned road and go looking for my own life, my own teachers, and my own answers.

The Search

The dark, and the climb out

There were lost years too, addiction, confusion, and the slow work of healing. I do not hide them. They are where much of my understanding was born.

The Road

Travelling to learn

I moved across Nepal, India, and Cambodia, hitchhiking, living on farms and in ashrams, learning yoga, Ayurveda, and how other communities truly live.

2013 onward

The Joon Project begins

I gathered friends and shared a simple idea, a movement to research and redesign how we live with each other, with nature, and with technology.

2016 to 2019

Maya Universe Academy

I arrived as a volunteer and became Principal, weaving yoga, meditation, and the arts into daily school life in Udayapur.

2020 to 2023

Hamro Orphanage

As managing advisor I helped turn an institutional orphanage into a warmer, family style, regenerative home.

2023

Permaculture at HASERA

I completed the International Permaculture Design Course at Patlekhet, deepening the regenerative thread that runs through all my work.

2026

Mery & Khem

I co founded a life design studio with Merina in Damak, bringing brand, digital, and life to one table.

04 · Services

How I can help you

Everything I offer is based on natural law and regenerative methods, to bring balance and harmony back to your life. Broken down by who I work with.

For Individuals

You are one person navigating life, feeling stuck or wanting to grow. Whether the friction is inside you or around you, I work alongside you to find the root and move toward a better version of your life.

  1. Deep conversation partner. Talk about anything without judgment, philosophy, love, trauma, spirituality, or goals. I listen, reflect, and help you think more clearly.
  2. Daily life companion. A meal, a walk, a coffee, a day out. Sometimes you need genuine company, not advice.
  3. Health, routine & Ayurveda. Daily routines, seasonal eating, micro habits, and time rooted in Ayurvedic principles.
  4. Astrology & self understanding. Your birth chart and patterns, and what they reveal about your character and direction.
  5. Vastu Shastra. Reading and redesigning your living space so flow and direction bring balance to your day.
  6. Yoga, meditation & physical practice. Yoga teacher, meditation guide, or training partner in football, MMA, swimming, and more.
  7. Outdoor & adventure companion. Hikes, treks, camping, and travel, teaching practical skills and reconnecting you with nature.
  8. Personal brand & digital presence. A clear, authentic public identity that reflects who you truly are.
  9. Life design & purpose. Understanding your body type and purpose, and building a realistic plan, step by step.
  10. Hands on & DIY. Practical home tasks, basic plumbing, carpentry, painting, and building. Grounding and often therapeutic.

For Families

When the whole family is ready to work together, I come in as a neutral observer and guide, to solve deep rooted problems, build unity, and design a shared vision.

  1. Family problem solving & mediation. Naming recurring conflicts and facilitating honest conversations that lead to real resolution.
  2. Unity & responsibility design. Distributing roles to suit each person's strengths, reducing resentment, building cooperation.
  3. Home & garden design. Eco architecture and permaculture gardens that the whole family benefits from.
  4. Family business planning. Aligning vision and clarifying roles so family and business can mix without tension.
  5. Future planning together. A shared long term plan for life, land, education, and community, rooted in natural law.

For Businesses

I enter a business the way a researcher enters a system. I observe quietly, map friction, and find what drains energy, money, and morale. An insider observer, not an outside consultant.

  1. Observation & friction mapping. Time inside your operations to find losses, loopholes, and hidden drains numbers do not reveal.
  2. Team training & culture. Communication, accountability, and a working culture with clarity and shared purpose.
  3. Branding, marketing & social media. Brand identity, strategy, content, and a team that can run it independently.
  4. Ethical & regenerative design. A profitable model that is not exploitative, built for the long term.
  5. Supplier, vendor & market research. The right partners and opportunities, so you decide rather than react.

For Nonprofits & Movements

If you are building something better with a genuine mission, I offer my time, research, and hands as a companion and collaborator.

  1. Research & planning partner. Understanding the systems you want to change and building a grounded long term strategy.
  2. Community design & events. Gatherings and activities, from concept and logistics to facilitation and follow up.
  3. Content, filming & storytelling. Giving your mission a clear, compelling voice that reaches the right people.
  4. Regenerative infrastructure. Permaculture, eco architecture, and food systems that make a project self sustaining.

A note. All my work is natural law based and regenerative. I will only offer paths and advice that do not harm human evolution or nature. So much of it may be radical, the bitter truth, and far from mainstream teaching. Be ready for it.

05 · Process

How the process works

Four phases. One clear path toward truth and transformation.

01

Deconstruction. The truth.

We read and understand this website together. Then you fill the form and book an hour long video call. If the problem is solved there, good. If not, we arrange a short visit to your place.

02

Identification. The friction.

I live closely with you for a week, observing routines, environments, food, work, posture, sleep, and everything in between. That is what you hired me for. I will be your best friend and advisor through it.

03

Design. The blueprint.

I suggest small, specific changes, one at a time. Nothing overwhelming. Only what is necessary, rooted in natural law, Ayurveda, spatial intelligence, and systems thinking.

04

Implementation. The reality.

If it needs more time, I stay as long as you need. The journey does not end at a plan. It ends when the change is real and felt in your daily life.

06 · Ethics

How I work, and with whom

I work with people who are genuinely ready to change. The work I do is not for everyone, and it is not designed to be easy. It is designed to be true.

I will be present, honest, and direct. I will not soften the message to make you comfortable, and I will not stretch the engagement to take more fees. If something will not work for you, I will tell you. If we are not the right fit, I will say so before we begin. I expect the same in return. Honest conversation, real effort, and a genuine intention to change.

The Vow of Implementation

Every engagement begins with your promise to actually apply what we agree on. I work for balance, not for talk.

On commitment. This work requires real effort from both sides. If you choose not to follow more than three of the agreed recommendations, the engagement will need to end. This is not a punishment. It is a boundary that protects both your time and mine.

Fees are non refundable once the work has begun. I ask only that you come with an open mind and a real intention to change.
